01Why now

Career Counselling Is the Most Under-Served Profession in Indian Education

The demand already exists in every district — unmet. Becoming a trained counsellor now means entering a field with almost no local competition and a policy tailwind behind it.

1 : 30,000

A demand-supply gap this wide is rare

India averages one counsellor for every 30,000 students against a global standard of 1:250. That gap is the single largest untapped professional opportunity in Indian education — and Tamil Nadu is wide open.

NEP 2020

Guidance is now mandatory — but unstaffed

Every school now carries a career-guidance requirement it cannot meet. Trained counsellors walk into a market where institutions need them and have nobody qualified to hire.

₹40–80k /mo

Real, flexible income

A counsellor with 2–3 school or college tie-ups typically earns ₹40,000–₹80,000 a month. Individual sessions in Tamil Nadu run ₹500–₹2,500. Work full-time, part-time, or as a second income.

AI

The AI advantage is here first

Thisai assesses a student and generates a personalised career roadmap in minutes. Counsellors who use AI build more trust with parents and serve more students than anyone relying on 20-year-old questionnaires.
