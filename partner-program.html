08The 2026 landscape

Career Counselling Is Being Rewritten by AI

The role of a career counsellor is shifting from simply helping a student pick a course to guiding their entire education-and-career journey. With information now instant, students don't need someone to look things up — they need someone to make sense of it all with them.

The counsellors who thrive will combine human expertise, technology and AI to guide students across career selection, education roadmaps, skill-building, admissions and portfolios. The future belongs to those who evolve from career advisors into career architects.

Thisai is built for exactly this future — and this program puts you at the front of it, in Tamil Nadu, in your own language.
